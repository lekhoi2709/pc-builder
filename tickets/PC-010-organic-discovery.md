# PC-010 — Help new buyers discover useful PC guidance

- **Priority:** P2 · **Status:** Backlog · **Epic:** Reach and acquisition · **Depends on:** PC-001 and trustworthy catalog data

## User story

As someone searching online for help choosing PC parts, I want useful Vietnamese and English guidance that connects to relevant products or a build, so that I can learn and continue planning in one place.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a guide targets a specific buyer question, **when** I open it from search, **then** it answers the question with dated, sourced, understandable guidance and a clear next action.
- **Given** a guide recommends a product or configuration, **when** its price, compatibility, or availability may change, **then** the page explains the date/source and links to current product data.
- **Given** a guide or product category is indexed by search engines, **when** they parse the page, **then** each route has a unique title, description, canonical URL, and useful page content rather than a generic app title.
- **Given** a visitor arrives directly at a localized route, **when** the page loads, **then** language, navigation, and metadata match that route.
- **Given** analytics are enabled, **when** a visitor uses a guide's primary action, **then** the product can measure guide-to-catalog/build progression without collecting unnecessary personal data.

## Notes

Start with high-intent topics the product can answer accurately (budget-based builds, compatibility basics, part selection). Avoid SEO filler or unverified benchmark/price claims.
