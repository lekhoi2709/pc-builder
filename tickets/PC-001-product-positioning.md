# PC-001 — Make the product promise match the real product

- **Priority:** P0 · **Status:** Backlog · **Epic:** Trust and first use

## User story

As a first-time visitor, I want to understand who PC Builder helps and what I can do here, so that I can decide whether to browse parts or plan a build without being misled.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** a visitor opens the home page in Vietnamese or English, **when** they read the headline and first screen, **then** it explains the real service and intended audience in that language.
- **Given** a page mentions price, stock, compatibility, assembly, warranty, support, AI, or shipping, **when** that claim is not backed by current product functionality or an operational commitment, **then** it is removed or rewritten as a clearly labeled future capability.
- **Given** the product owner confirms the recommended planning-tool direction or chooses another direction, **when** the decision is recorded, **then** the home page, About page, app metadata, and README use the same positioning.
- **Given** a user reaches the home page, **when** they see the primary call to action, **then** its label describes a destination/action that exists today.

## Notes

The direction in tickets/00-product-review.md is a recommendation to validate. Do not implement store checkout or operational promises as part of this ticket.
